Category Expansions to Pursue
🍷 Adult Social Craft

Craft activities designed for adult gatherings—not solo, not kids. "The activity IS the party." Wine + paint nights proved the model; at-home version underserved.

Opportunities: Paint-your-own glassware, pottery painting sets, candle-making party kits, terrarium building
🏠 Everyday Hosting

Products for non-milestone gatherings. Wednesday wine night. Sunday brunch. Book club. Current party aisle is birthday/holiday dominated.

Opportunities: "Tuesday Night" table kits, cocktail hour accessories, outdoor entertaining, conversation starters
✨ Home Decor Crossover

Products that live in the home year-round but serve party function. Blur decor and celebration. Unlocks TJX HomeGoods real estate.

Opportunities: Modular shelf displays, design-forward serving ware, entertaining vessels, decorative trays
🚫
Trends to IGNORE
Kids' Experience Party Kits

Violates adult-first positioning. Competes at commodity price. TJX party section isn't kids' destination. Confuses brand in buyer's mind.

Licensed Character Extensions

Margin destruction from fees. Renting equity, not building it. One bad movie = stranded inventory. HOP's value is original design.

Single-Use Themed Tableware

Commodity race to bottom. Sustainability backlash. No brand loyalty. TJX won't carry (no treasure hunt value). Amazon/dollar store dominates.

✓
Product Decision Filter

Before greenlighting ANY product, ask:

  1. Would a 32-year-old woman hosting brunch buy this? (Adult-first test)
  2. Would a TJX buyer put this in HomeGoods? (Off-price scalability test)
  3. Could we sell a refill/expansion/sequel? (System logic test)
  4. Does this create social content opportunity? (Brand amplification test)
  5. Is this defensible without licensing fees? (Ownership test)
