Brand Positioning & Whitespace

THREADIES

Slow craft anchor for meditative, tactile, display-worthy fiber craft. The anti-screen, anti-hustle brand extension.

Whitespace We Own: Adult-first, decor-worthy fiber craft at $12-24 TJX price points. Punch needle with display-ready frames.

MUDDIES

Expressive, tactile, paint-based craft line. "Therapeutic mess with a beautiful result." Adult play, not art class.

Whitespace We Own: Textured, dimensional paint craft at $10-20. Famous art reimagined for DIY (Van Gogh, Monet proven winners).

PIXELPOP

Dopamine-driven, visually bold, collectible craft. Satisfying, repetitive experiences with high-impact visual payoff.

Whitespace We Own: 3D display pixel art, occasion-based kits (Date Night, Girls Night), adult-positioned decor-worthy pixel art.

3 Ideas to NOT Pursue

Character/Animal Crochet Kits (Woobles-style)
The Woobles owns this completely. $5M+ in sales, Shark Tank exposure, Walmart distribution. We can't out-cute them. Pivot to decor-first fiber instead.
Flat Paint-by-Numbers (Generic Landscapes)
Market is saturated with $9.99 Amazon knockoffs. No differentiation possible. Our textured approach is the only path to premium positioning.
Single-Use Party Favors or Disposable Plastic Decor
Violates brand exclusions. TJX is moving toward sustainability. HomeGoods 2026 trends emphasize "authentic materials." Don't chase dying formats.
