Trends to IGNORE
Licensed Character Dependency

Margin compression from fees. Renting someone else's equity. One bad movie = stranded inventory. Build ownable aesthetics instead.

LED/Digital-Enhanced Decor

High unit cost, low margin. Reliability/returns issues. Doesn't fit TJX price thresholds. Battery disposal contradicts sustainability.

Ultra-Premium Chateaucore

Doesn't scale to mass price points. Niche Pinterest aesthetic. Execution cost prohibitive for paper goods. Own "elevated casual" instead.

Micro-Wedding Supplies

Different buyer, different channel. Extremely seasonal. One-time purchase, no velocity. Target/TJX not wedding destinations.

Slime/Sensory Overload Kits

Market saturated, commoditized. Cleanup/mess perception = barrier. Returns high. Muddies should be result-oriented, not "just mess."

Single-Use Plastic Novelty

Regulatory pressure accelerating. Brand risk with eco-conscious consumers. Target curating away from it. Doesn't differentiate.
